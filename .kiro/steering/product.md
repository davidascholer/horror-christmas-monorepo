# Product steering

## Mission and audience

Horror Christmas is the home for Christmas horror culture: media, folklore,
collecting, seasonal traditions, gifts, and discovery. Primary users are
Christmas horror enthusiasts; collectors/shoppers and seasonal visitors are
secondary audiences. The brand promise is **Everything Christmas Horror. One
Home.**

## Product thesis

**Collections are the product.** Movies, characters, products, franchises,
and tags are the structured content that collections connect into curated
experiences. Optimize navigation, homepage content, search, internal links,
and editorial investment for collection-first discovery.

Collections are human-curated, ordered, and written for a purpose. They are
not tags, categories, automatically generated lists, or search results.
Curator notes explain why a collection exists, who it is for, where to start,
and what to explore next.

## MVP scope and validation

The MVP validates that a dedicated collection-led destination encourages
multi-page exploration, seasonal return visits, anonymous watchlists, search
use, and contextual affiliate clicks. Success is meaningful collection
engagement, not raw traffic alone. Commerce supports fandom; it must not turn
the product into an affiliate farm, product catalog, or marketplace.

The public MVP has no visitor accounts, reviews, ratings, comments, discussions,
forums, community features, user-created collections, recommendation engine,
marketplace transactions, or mobile application. Keep future mobile clients
possible through stable API contracts without implementing mobile apps now.
